This owner-only report reads the existing Google workbook and compares candidate customer names with native HQ records without importing, linking or editing anything. It does not establish customer identity from a shared name.

Google Sheets stays authoritative for unmigrated history. New native HQ records stay authoritative only for their own data. The comparison does not change either system; saving a review candidate writes only an inactive, owner-only HQ proposal and an audit event. Never assume a completed job is paid.

Manual action only. Scans Jobs and Customers from row 5 through row 1000. Requires active owner consent. No background sync, import, email, booking or payment.
