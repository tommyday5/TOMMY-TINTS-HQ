Record or correct a reported allocation

Use a specific payment reference when available. HQ rejects another active allocation on the same invoice with the same date, amount and reference (ignoring letter case). Distinct dates or amounts, and entries without references, still require your own duplicate checks. This is not bank verification.

To correct an amount or invoice, reverse its existing allocation and add a new one. Past revisions are retained. Reversing does not refund anyone or change an external account.
